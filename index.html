About

Who's in
the booth

I'm Jason, doing voiceover and audio production out of my treated studio in California, full time, Monday through Friday.

If years of skateboarding taught me anything, it's tenacity. No matter the pain or the frustration, getting it just right is the best feeling in the world and I've carried that into the audio world. Started my audio engineering journey in 2011 at the Los Angeles Recording School. After that, went off to skate in a couple videos and play in bands. Now, after a good bit of fun, I'm here to help bring your project to life.

I've traded broken bones for broken strings, long filming sessions for long recording and mixing sessions. The joy of finally rolling away after hours of struggling is the same feeling I get after nailing a session. I'm here till you love it.
